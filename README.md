# Kairos

Kairos is a centralized team project-management and **collaboration** desktop application created by Technical Difficulties

Most small teams and student groups manage projects across a fragmented, chaotic patchwork of tools—chat apps for discussions, shared drives for files, spreadsheets for deadlines, and separate boards for tasks. This leads to missed deadlines, duplicated effort, and lost resources.

## Meetings

Start or schedule a Jitsi video meeting from any chat (**Meeting...**), from a team (**New team meeting...** on the Teams page) or from the Dashboard (**New meeting...**). Everyone in the chat - or on the team - is invited and can Accept, mark Tentative or Decline; the person who created the meeting is its host, and only they (or an admin) can cancel it. Upcoming meetings are listed on the Dashboard with a **Join** button and shown in blue on its calendar.

Meetings open in your web browser on [meet.jit.si](https://meet.jit.si). Jitsi asks whoever opens a meeting room first to sign in (Google, GitHub or Facebook) and makes them the meeting's moderator - the one who can mute people, turn on the lobby or remove someone. Your browser remembers that sign-in; use **Profile → Sign in to Jitsi...** to do it before your first meeting, and set the name you join with and whether you join muted or with your camera off. An admin can point Kairos at a different Jitsi server in **Server Settings**.
